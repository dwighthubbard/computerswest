# FAQ

## What is 3D printing?
3‑D printing, also known as additive manufacturing, builds objects layer by layer from digital models.

## What materials can be used?
Common filaments include PLA, ABS, PETG, and TPU. Resin printers use liquid photopolymer resins.

## How do I choose a printer?
Consider your budget, required resolution, build volume, and material compatibility.

## What is support structure?
Supports are temporary scaffolds printed to hold overhanging parts; they are removed after printing.

## How do I prepare a model for printing?
Use slicing software to generate G‑code, set layer height, infill, and support settings.
