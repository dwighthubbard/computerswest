# 3D Printing Special Interest Group (SIG)

!!! info "Next Class"
    July 6th, 2026 - [Introduction to 3D Printing](classes/introduction/index.md)

Explore the world of 3D printing, from design to fabrication, and share knowledge on best practices and innovations in the field.

## SIG Meetings

- Regular meeting times and location are listed on the club calendar. Check the club site or SIG board for the next session and presentation topic.
- Meetings typically include demonstrations, member Q&A, and hands-on help.
- Some meetings will have a class that covers a specific common topic.

## 12-Week Class Rotation

Each class is designed to fit within a 50-minute SIG meeting slot.

| Week | Class                                                                                                           | Description                                                                                            |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | [Introduction to 3D Printing](classes/introduction/index.md)                                                    | Basic workflow, vocabulary, printer setup, first print, and when to own or outsource.                  |
| 2    | [Getting Models](classes/Getting%20Models/index.md) Coming Soon                                                 | Finding printable models, evaluating files, and understanding licenses.                                |
| 3    | [Slicing](classes/Slicing/index.md)  Coming Soon                                                                | Turning a model into printer instructions using slicer profiles, settings, and preview.                |
| 4    | [Materials](classes/Materials/index.md) Coming Soon                                                             | Choosing PLA, PETG, TPU, resin, and specialty materials for the job.                                   |
| 5    | [Parametric Design](classes/Parametric%20Design/index.md) Coming Soon                                           | Customizing designs by changing dimensions and constraints.                                            |
| 6    | [AI Design](classes/AI%20Design/index.md) Coming Soon                                                           | Using AI for ideas, prompts, simple design assistance, and review checklists.                          |
| 7    | [Common Problems and Solutions](classes/Common%20Problems%20and%20Solutions/index.md) Coming Soon               | Diagnosing first-layer issues, stringing, warping, clogs, weak parts, and other common failures.       |
| 8    | [Designing For 3D Printing](classes/Designing%20For%203D%20Printing/index.md) Coming Soon                       | Designing parts that print reliably with good orientation, tolerances, strength, and support strategy. |
| 9    | [Functional Parts and Repairs](classes/Functional%20Parts%20and%20Repairs/index.md) Coming Soon                 | Measuring, designing, and testing practical repair parts and utility prints.                           |
| 10   | [Post Processing](classes/Post%20Processing/index.md) Coming Soon                                               | Removing supports, sanding, gluing, adding hardware, and finishing prints safely.                      |
| 11   | [Maintenance and Calibration](classes/Maintenance%20and%20Calibration/index.md) Coming Soon                     | Routine printer care, calibration prints, cleaning, inspection, and logs.                              |
| 12   | [3D Scanning and Reverse Engineering](classes/3D%20Scanning%20and%20Reverse%20Engineering/index.md) Coming Soon | Capturing real objects, understanding scan limits, and recreating functional parts.                    |
