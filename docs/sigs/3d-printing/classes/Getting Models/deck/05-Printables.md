Printables is the 3D creator website for Prusa Research one of the designers
and sellers of 3d printers.  The site has a wide variety of models available.

Printables frequently runs [contests](https://www.printables.com/contest) to create models where people can win filament and 3d printers that 
Prusa makes.  As a result they have a lot of unique models that where entered in the 
contents.

They also have payed models from various talented designers.
