Founded in 2008, Thingiverse is One of the first 3D model libraries. It is still one 
of the largest and most popular 3d print libraries. 

It is a free library of models that anyone can upload to and download from. 

You can find models for almost anything on Thingiverse, from toys to tools to 
replacement parts.  However, Thingiverse stipulates that users must not 
include content that "contributes to the creation of weapons, illegal materials, 
or is otherwise objectionable."

In February 2026 Thingiverse was acquired by [MyMiniFactory](05-MyMiniFactory.md).
