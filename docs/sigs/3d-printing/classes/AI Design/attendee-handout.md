# AI Design: Attendee Handout

## Good AI Uses

- Brainstorming project ideas.
- Making a checklist of measurements.
- Explaining CAD concepts.
- Drafting simple OpenSCAD code.
- Comparing material options.
- Reviewing whether a design may be hard to print.

## Poor AI Uses

- Safety-critical parts.
- Load-bearing parts without engineering review.
- Exact-fit replacement parts without measurement and testing.
- Assuming a generated visual image is printable.

## Prompt Checklist

Include:

- What the part is for.
- Key measurements in millimeters.
- Material.
- Printer type.
- Strength or flexibility needs.
- Whether supports should be avoided.
- Any holes, clearances, or mounting points.

## Rule

Treat AI output as a draft. Measure, inspect, slice, and test before relying on it.
