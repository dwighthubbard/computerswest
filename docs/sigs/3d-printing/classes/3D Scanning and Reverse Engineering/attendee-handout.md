# 3D Scanning and Reverse Engineering: Attendee Handout

## Scanning Works Best For

- Decorative objects.
- Organic shapes.
- Objects where exact dimensions are not critical.
- Reference geometry for a new design.

## Scanning Works Poorly For

- Shiny objects.
- Transparent objects.
- Very dark objects.
- Thin edges.
- Precision mechanical parts.
- Parts with hidden internal features.

## Reverse Engineering Workflow

1. Identify what the part must do.
2. Measure key dimensions with calipers.
3. Sketch the functional features.
4. Model the part in CAD.
5. Print a small fit test.
6. Revise before printing the final part.

## Key Idea

A scan gives you shape. A replacement part needs function, fit, material, and strength.
