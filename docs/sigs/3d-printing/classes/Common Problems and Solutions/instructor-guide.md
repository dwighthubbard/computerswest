# Instructor Guide: Common Problems and Solutions

## Audience

Beginners who have printed at least once or who want to understand why prints fail.

## Key Points

- A good diagnosis starts with observing the print, not guessing.
- The first layer affects the rest of the print.
- Bed cleanliness, Z offset, temperature, and filament condition cause many problems.
- Slicer preview can catch support, orientation, and thin-wall issues before printing.
- Change one variable at a time or you will not know what fixed the problem.

## Problem Categories

Poor bed adhesion
: Clean build plate, correct Z offset, correct bed temperature, brim if needed.

Stringing
: Dry filament, temperature tuning, retraction settings, travel speed.

Warping
: Material choice, bed temperature, enclosure, brim, avoid drafts.

Layer shift
: Check belts, collisions, loose pulleys, speed, and mechanical obstruction.

Clogged nozzle
: Check filament path, nozzle temperature, debris, heat creep, and nozzle condition.

Weak parts
: Check orientation, walls, material, temperature, and layer adhesion.

## Demo Flow

1. Show a failed print or photo.
2. Ask attendees what they observe.
3. Name the symptom.
4. List likely causes.
5. Choose the first safe fix.
6. Explain what not to change yet.
