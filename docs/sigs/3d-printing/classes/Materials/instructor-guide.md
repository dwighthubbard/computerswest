# Instructor Guide: Materials

## Audience

Beginners and intermediate users who need help choosing material for a project.

## Key Points

- Material choice should follow the use case.
- PLA is easy and good for many indoor decorative and light-duty parts.
- PETG is tougher and more heat resistant than PLA, but can be stringier.
- TPU is flexible and useful for grips, bumpers, and soft parts.
- ABS and ASA can be useful but usually require enclosure and ventilation.
- Resin can produce fine detail but requires chemical handling and post-processing.
- Specialty filaments can be abrasive, brittle, expensive, or harder to print.

## Demo Flow

1. Show a simple comparison table.
2. Pass around or display labeled material samples.
3. Match sample objects to use cases.
4. Explain storage and moisture problems.
5. Discuss safety and ventilation at a high level.
6. Close with a material selection checklist.

## Common Questions

What material should beginners use?
: PLA, unless the part has a specific requirement PLA cannot meet.

Can I print outdoor parts in PLA?
: Sometimes, but PETG or ASA may be better depending on heat, sun, and load.

Do I need a filament dryer?
: Not always, but wet filament can cause poor surface finish, popping, stringing, and weak prints.
