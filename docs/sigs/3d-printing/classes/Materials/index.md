# Materials

This class introduces common 3D printing materials, what they are good for, and what safety or printer requirements they may have.

## Class Goal

By the end of the class, attendees should understand:

- Why PLA is usually the easiest starting material.
- When PETG, TPU, ABS, ASA, resin, or specialty materials may be useful.
- How heat, flexibility, sunlight, strength, detail, and safety affect material choice.
- Why some materials require ventilation, enclosures, drying, or services.
- How filament storage affects print quality.

## Suggested Demonstration

Show physical samples of PLA, PETG, TPU, silk PLA, wood-filled PLA, and a failed wet-filament print if available. Compare stiffness, surface finish, flexibility, and intended use.
