# Materials: Attendee Handout

## Common Materials

PLA
: Easiest beginner material. Good for indoor decorative and light functional prints.

PETG
: Tougher than PLA and more heat resistant. Good for many practical parts.

TPU
: Flexible material for bumpers, feet, grips, and soft parts.

ABS / ASA
: Stronger heat-resistant materials, but often require enclosure and ventilation.

Resin
: High detail, but requires chemical handling, washing, curing, gloves, and disposal care.

Specialty filaments
: Silk, wood-filled, carbon fiber-filled, glow, metal-filled, and other blends may need special settings or hardened nozzles.

## Selection Questions

- Will the part be indoors or outdoors?
- Will it get hot?
- Does it need to bend?
- Does it need to hold weight?
- Does appearance matter more than strength?
- Is the material safe and practical for the available printer?

## Storage Tip

Keep filament dry and sealed when not in use, especially PETG, TPU, nylon, and many specialty materials.
