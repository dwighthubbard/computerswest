# Designing For 3D Printing: Attendee Handout

## Design Checklist

- Can the part print without supports?
- Is the strongest direction aligned with the load?
- Are walls thick enough?
- Do holes and slots have clearance?
- Can the part be split into simpler pieces?
- Is a small test print possible?

## Concepts

Overhang
: Part of a model that extends outward without material below it.

Bridge
: A short span printed across a gap.

Tolerance
: Extra space added so parts fit together after printing.

Layer direction
: The direction layers are stacked; parts are often weakest between layers.

## Practical Advice

Design for the printer you have, not the perfect printer you wish you had.
