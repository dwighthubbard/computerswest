# Instructor Guide: Post Processing

## Audience

Attendees who have printed objects and want them to look better or work better.

## Key Points

- Let the bed cool before forcing a print loose.
- Support removal can damage small details if rushed.
- Deburring tools, files, and sandpaper are useful but require care.
- Dust and fumes should be managed safely.
- Heat-set inserts can make stronger threaded connections.
- Resin prints require gloves, washing, curing, and proper disposal.

## Demo Flow

1. Show a print fresh off the bed.
2. Demonstrate safe part removal or explain bed cooling.
3. Remove supports from a sample.
4. Deburr a rough edge.
5. Show sanding stages or finished examples.
6. Demonstrate or describe heat-set inserts.
7. Discuss paint, glue, and safety.

## Common Questions

Should I sand PLA?
: You can, but it takes patience. Start with coarse grit only when needed and work upward.

What glue works?
: It depends on material. Cyanoacrylate works for many PLA parts; other materials may need different adhesives.

Are resin prints safe after curing?
: Fully cured resin is safer to handle, but uncured resin and wash liquid require protective handling and proper disposal.
