# Instructor Guide: Functional Parts and Repairs

## Audience

Attendees interested in practical repairs and utility parts rather than decorative prints.

## Key Points

- Not every broken part should be replaced with plastic.
- Measure the job the part must do, not just the old part's shape.
- Calipers are essential for fit.
- Functional parts usually need attention to orientation, walls, and material.
- Small test pieces reduce wasted time.
- Avoid safety-critical uses unless the design is properly engineered.

## Demo Flow

1. Present a broken or missing part.
2. Identify the function: hold, space, guide, cover, label, grip, or adapt.
3. Measure key dimensions with calipers.
4. Sketch the part and mark tolerances.
5. Discuss material choice.
6. Discuss print orientation for strength.
7. Plan a fit test.

## Common Questions

Can 3D printed parts replace metal?
: Sometimes for light-duty uses, but not automatically. Load, heat, wear, and safety matter.

How accurate are printed parts?
: Accuracy depends on printer calibration, material, orientation, and design clearance.

What should I print first?
: A small fit test or critical feature, not the entire final part.
