# Functional Parts and Repairs: Attendee Handout

## Good Candidates

- Spacers
- Cable guides
- Drawer organizers
- Light-duty brackets
- Knobs
- Feet and bumpers
- Covers and caps
- Templates and jigs

## Poor Candidates

- High-heat parts near engines, ovens, or lamps.
- Safety-critical parts.
- Parts that hold people, pets, vehicles, or heavy loads.
- Food-contact parts unless material and process are appropriate.

## Measurement Checklist

- Overall length, width, and height.
- Hole diameter and spacing.
- Wall thickness.
- Clearance needed for movement.
- Load direction.
- Temperature and environment.

## Repair Workflow

1. Understand the function.
2. Measure the key features.
3. Choose material.
4. Design or customize the part.
5. Print a small test.
6. Adjust and print final version.
